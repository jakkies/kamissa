The Approach

Quantum Energy Coaching

Quantum Energy Coaching (QEC) is the approach used by Kamissa Coaching to work with limiting beliefs and behaviour patterns at the subconscious level of mind.

QEC was developed by Dr Melanie Salmon and draws on approaches including neuroscience and Gestalt psychotherapy.

  1. 01

  4. 04

    Install

    Reinforce the new belief at a subconscious level.

Kintsugi

There is beauty in what has been mended.

Kintsugi is the Japanese art of repairing broken pottery with gold. Rather than hiding the cracks, the repair becomes part of the object’s story.

Our experiences can leave cracks. Loss, disappointment, pain and the beliefs we develop along the way can influence how we see ourselves.

But our story doesn’t have to end there.
