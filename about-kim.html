About Kim

Meet Kim

A journey of healing, purpose and transformation — and an invitation to walk part of yours together.

“My calling is to walk alongside people as they discover what God has dreamed for them.”

My Calling

Walking alongside people

I believe meaningful transformation begins with genuine connection. My role is to create a safe space where you can explore, listen to what is happening within you, and discover the beliefs that may be limiting your wellbeing.

I am not here to tell you who you should be. I am here to walk alongside you while you rediscover who you already are.

My Journey

I have walked through the space between.

My own journey has included grief and loss — seasons where the road felt long and the way forward unclear, and where I lost something of my own voice along the way.

Slowly, through healing, honesty and unexpected grace, I found my voice again. Music became part of that return, and so did a deep desire to help others find their way back to themselves.

What I learned in that space between is the reason Kamissa exists.

Kintsugi

There is beauty in what has been mended.

Kintsugi is the Japanese art of repairing broken pottery with gold. The repair is not hidden — it becomes part of the story.

Our experiences leave cracks. Loss, disappointment and the beliefs we form along the way shape how we see ourselves. But the story does not have to end there.

Mended does not mean lesser. Often it means more honest, and more beautiful.

Faith & Healing

Held, not prescribed.

My own faith shapes how I see people: with dignity, compassion and hope. It informs the way I work, but it is never imposed.

Clients of any belief or none are warmly welcome, and you are always free to set the tone of our conversations.

How I Work

Unhurried, honest and safe.

Sessions are calm and conversational. You choose what to bring and how deep to go. I listen carefully, ask gentle questions and guide the process — nothing is rushed.

Everything shared stays confidential.

Why Kamissa?

A place of Sweet Water.

This work began as Liminil — named for the liminal space, that uncomfortable space between what was and what is not yet.

Kamissa is what you find along that journey: an oasis. A place of sweet water where you can stop, breathe, reflect, restore and continue refreshed.

I would be honoured to walk alongside you.
